# Day 35 — Freetime and the first fully timed Sprechen mock

**Phase:** 3 — Output | **Total:** 2h15 | **New cards:** 32

## Why this matters

Today is your first fully timed Sprechen mock, all three Teile back to back. It exists to
surface, before day 42's graded mock, exactly which Teil falls apart under a clock.

## Core concepts

A monologue you can only deliver while reading is not yet a monologue. Today's recording
and today's timed Sprechen mock both test the same thing: can you produce it from memory,
under time pressure, without the page.

### Block 1 — Anki (25 min)

Clear both decks' review queues. Take today's 32 new cards as the next contiguous slice:
words 97–128, all of them inside `t2_verbs` (`stellen`, `legen`, `vergessen`,
`versuchen`, `sparen`, `aufwachsen`, `einsteigen`, `anbieten`). Today's slice sits wholly
in one group; tomorrow's will cross into the next.

Every `t2_verbs` card carries its Perfekt form on the back, in brackets after the English
— `to stay (Perfekt: ist geblieben)`. Learn it as part of the word, not as an extra: at A1
there is no rule that lets you build the past tense from the infinitive, so the form has to
come with the verb or it does not come at all. The `ist` or `hat` in front tells you which
auxiliary that verb takes. Say the whole thing aloud each time the card comes up —
infinitive, English, then Perfekt — the way you drilled Tier 1 Perfekt on days 18–20.

### Block 2 — Input (40 min)

Open `vocabulary/wordlists/13_freetime.md` and read it once silently, once aloud. Then
re-shadow the freetime-themed Nicos Weg episode at
https://learngerman.dw.com/en/nicos-weg/c-36519789, full native speed, no subtitles.

### Block 3 — Output (40 min)

Speak a 90-second monologue on `13_freetime` — what you do, when, with whom — using at
least one modal form from your `t2_modals` cards — `ich möchte`, `ich kann` — built out
into a full sentence (`Ich möchte gern …`, `Ich kann gut …`). Draft it once,
then deliver and record it live without reading. Play it back once.

### Block 4 — Exam drill (30 min)

Run all three Sprechen Teile from the second Übungssatz, timed, back to back, from memory
— use `templates/sprechen_scripts.md` only to check yourself afterward, not during. Record
all three Teile. This is your first full timed Sprechen rehearsal; treat it as a rough
draft of day 42.

## Exercises

1. Say your five Teil 3 request frames from memory, one per real object in the room. — **Hint:** the frames are fixed sentence shapes — swap only the object, never the shape. — **Solution sketch:** `Können Sie bitte das Fenster öffnen?`
2. Build one sentence combining `möchte gern` with a freetime activity. — **Hint:** `möchte` behaves like a modal — the second verb goes to the end, in its plain infinitive form. — **Solution sketch:** `Samstags möchte ich gern Fußball spielen.`
3. Time your Teil 2 answers against the theme table in `templates/sprechen_scripts.md`. — **Hint:** each Antwort should be a full sentence, not a single word — a one-word answer under-delivers on a Teil 2 point. — **Solution sketch:** if you answered `Bücher` alone, expand it to `Ich lese gern Bücher.`

## Anti-patterns / Common mistakes

- Reading directly from `templates/sprechen_scripts.md` during today's timed mock instead
  of speaking from memory — the real exam gives you no script, and today is when you find
  out, not gently, whether it's actually memorised.
- Skipping Teil 3 in the mock because requests feel awkward. Teil 3 is scored inside the
  same Sprechen marks as the other two Teile; avoiding it in practice guarantees avoiding
  it under real pressure too.
