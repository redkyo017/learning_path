# Day 09 — der/die/das and plural drilling

**Phase:** 1 — Engine | **Total:** 2h15 | **New cards:** 25

## Why this matters

Today's 25 cards sit wholly inside the home theme, and by now your Anki backlog almost certainly
contains a handful of nouns whose article you keep guessing instead of recalling. There
is no new grammar today — the entire day is deliberate, focused recall practice, because
`STRATEGY.md` names bare-noun learning as one of the costliest habits at this level, and
the fix is repetition, not a rule.

## Core concepts

German's three [Artikel](GLOSSARY.md#grammar) — `der`, `die`, `das` — sort nouns into
groups with no reliable logic behind them, and the [Plural](GLOSSARY.md#grammar) form is
equally unpredictable (`-e`, `-en`, `-er`, `-s`, an umlaut, or nothing added). Vietnamese
has neither feature, so there is no intuition to fall back on — every noun is memorised
as a fixed three-part chunk: article, noun, plural, exactly as it sits on the front of
its Anki card. Today's whole point is to test that chunk under recall pressure, out
loud, away from the card.

### Block 1 — Anki (25 min)

Clear the review queue, then study today's 25 new cards: theme 04_home, words
151–175 — a full slice inside the home theme, which opened yesterday.

### Block 2 — Input (40 min)

Open Nicos Weg: https://learngerman.dw.com/en/nicos-weg/c-36519789
Watch the episodes that introduce a batch of new everyday nouns — shopping, objects,
places. Watch each twice: once for gist, once shadowing every line aloud, and write down
each new noun with the article the episode gives it.

### Block 3 — Output (40 min)

Say article plus plural for 30 nouns from memory, drawn from every theme you've studied
so far (01_personal, 02_family, 03_time, 04_home — 175 words total). Do not look at the wordlist
until you've said all 30; only then check, and mark the ones you got wrong. Record the
full 30-noun run.

### Block 4 — Exam drill (30 min)

Open the Übungssatz PDF from day 1. Read Lesen Teil 2 under a timed limit — this part is
closer to a short schedule or opening-hours notice — and answer its questions before
checking. Note every time expression the text uses (a clock time, a day, an
opening-hours range), since that overlaps directly with the time theme you finished yesterday.

## Exercises

1. Say article and plural for five nouns you flagged as uncertain in Anki this week. — **Hint:** if you hesitate for more than two seconds, that is the signal to flag the card as a leech, not to keep guessing at it. — **Solution sketch:** e.g. `der Monat, die Monate; die Uhr, die Uhren; das Jahr, die Jahre` — say the chunk, don't isolate the noun.
2. Sort nine nouns from this week into three lists — `der`, `die`, `das` — three per list, purely from memory, then check. — **Hint:** don't try to find a pattern across the nine; there mostly isn't one at A1, and hunting for logic wastes the two seconds you should spend recalling instead. — **Solution sketch:** `der: der Tag, der Monat, der Balkon / die: die Woche, die Uhr, die Zeit / das: das Jahr, das Haus, das Zimmer` — adjust with your own nine, taken from words 1–175, and check against the wordlist.
3. Pick five nouns from today's new words (theme 04_home, words 151–175) and produce article plus plural aloud. — **Hint:** this is the same drill as Block 3, scoped to only today's newest, least-consolidated cards — expect a lower hit rate than yesterday's words. — **Solution sketch:** e.g. `der Garten, die Gärten; der Schrank, die Schränke; das Fenster, die Fenster` — say each pair aloud twice, then check the wordlist.

## Anti-patterns / Common mistakes

- Treating today as a low-effort day because "there's no new grammar." Article-plural recall has its own row in `runbook.md`'s troubleshooting table ("You keep forgetting der/die/das") — skipping this drill just because it isn't a new rule undercuts every theme you've studied so far.
- Checking the wordlist after every single noun instead of finishing a full batch first. Checking one at a time turns the drill into recognition (seeing the answer jog your memory) instead of production (recalling it cold), and only production is what the exam actually asks for.
