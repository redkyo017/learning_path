# Day 34 — Transcribe and correct yourself

**Phase:** 3 — Output | **Total:** 2h15 | **New cards:** 32

## Why this matters

A tutor is polite; a transcript is not. Writing down exactly what you said, sound by
sound, catches errors a kind listener lets slide — and it is free, repeatable, and
available every time you have a recording.

## Core concepts

Transcribe what you actually produced, not what you meant to say. The gap between the two
is the whole point of the exercise.

### Block 1 — Anki (25 min)

Clear both decks' review queues. Take today's 32 new cards as the next contiguous slice:
words 65–96. Rows 65–81 finish `t2_modals` (`du darfst`, `ich soll`, `ihr sollt`,
`ich möchte`, `wir möchten`) and rows 82–96 open `t2_verbs` (`machen`, `denken`,
`glauben`, `wissen`, `geben`, `bleiben`). The modal rows are bare conjugated forms, one
per person — a conjugation drill, not ready-made phrases; you supply the rest of the
sentence yourself.

### Block 2 — Input (40 min)

Reopen https://learngerman.dw.com/en/nicos-weg/c-36519789 and re-shadow a Phase 1 or
Phase 2 episode you have not yet re-shadowed this phase, at full native speed, no
subtitles. This is separate ear training from the transcription task below — do not skip
it just because Block 3 is also about your own voice today.

### Block 3 — Output (40 min)

Play your day-32 recording. Write down, word for word, what you actually said — not what
your draft script said. Compare the transcript to your original draft line by line. Mark
every gap: a dropped article, wrong verb position, a missing connector, a swallowed final
consonant. Circle the three worst.

### Block 4 — Exam drill (30 min)

Run the Schreiben module from the second Übungssatz
(https://www.goethe.de/en/spr/kup/prf/prf/sd1/ueb.html) under full timing — both the
Teil 1 form and the Teil 2 message. Check the Teil 2 message against its three required
points before you check anything else.

## Exercises

1. Transcribe one full sentence of your day-32 recording exactly as spoken, mistakes included. — **Hint:** write the sounds you hear, not the sentence you intended — the two are often different. — **Solution sketch:** spoken `Ich fahre nach Deutschland gern` transcribed exactly like that, error kept.
2. Fix the word-order error in that transcribed sentence. — **Hint:** `gern` sits directly after the verb, not at the end of the clause. — **Solution sketch:** `Ich fahre gern nach Deutschland.`
3. Find one dropped article in your transcript and correct it. — **Hint:** Vietnamese has no articles, so they are the first thing that vanishes under speaking pressure. — **Solution sketch:** `Ich fahre mit Zug` corrected to `Ich fahre mit dem Zug.`

## Anti-patterns / Common mistakes

- Transcribing the meaning you intended rather than the sounds you actually produced —
  this erases exactly the errors transcription exists to catch.
- Marking every deviation as equally serious. Spend your three circles on errors that
  would confuse a listener — wrong word order, a dropped verb — not minor accent wobbles.

## Minimum viable day

Block 1 + Block 3, transcribing only the first 30 seconds of the recording (45 min).
