# Day 42 — The full mock — gate 60

**Phase:** 3 — Output | **Total:** 2h15 | **New cards:** 0

## Why this matters

This is the day the Phase 3 gate gets tested: a full mock, all four modules, timed, and
the number decides what day 43 looks like. Nothing new gets introduced today — day 42
measures what is already installed, not what you can cram this morning.

## Core concepts

The four block labels below keep their usual names, but today's real time split is not
25/40/40/30. Anki and Input shrink to a quiet review; Output is rest, not production; the
full mock absorbs most of the day's 2h15.

### Block 1 — Anki (25 min)

Clear the Tier 1 and Tier 2 review queues — reviews only, zero new cards on either deck,
today and, for Tier 1, permanently. Day 42 introduces nothing new on purpose.

### Block 2 — Input (40 min)

Read back your day-31 mock score log in `progress_tracker.md` and your Anki leech list —
the highest-lapse cards — across both decks. Do not open any new material, vocabulary
list, or Nicos Weg episode today.

### Block 3 — Output (40 min)

Rest the voice. No italki call today, no recorded monologue — if a session would
otherwise land today, move it. Reread your Teil 1 script from `templates/sprechen_scripts.md`
silently, once, for structure only. Do not speak it aloud; save your voice for the mock's
Sprechen module.

### Block 4 — Exam drill (30 min)

Run the full mock: all four modules — Hören, Lesen, Schreiben, Sprechen — back to back,
under real timing, from the second Übungssatz you have used across days 32–41
(https://www.goethe.de/en/spr/kup/prf/prf/sd1/ueb.html). Record yourself for Sprechen
since there is no live partner today. Score each module, then sum them.

**On the supply of Übungssätze.** Today's mock spends the second official set, and there
are fewer free Übungssätze than this path schedules mocks — days 31, 42, 44, 46, 48 and
50. The durable rule for that shortage (use each set once, the ten-day wait before a
re-sit, scoring only the items you got wrong, marking a re-sit in `progress_tracker.md`,
and resting the 75+ booking gate on a first-sitting paper) lives in `runbook.md` §Mock
papers and the Übungssatz supply. Read it there before every mock day from 44 on. Today
needs only the first line of it: this is a first sitting, so score it straight.

**Gate: 60+.** If your total is 60 or above, log it in `progress_tracker.md`'s Mock log as
Mock 2 (day 42) and move to day 43 on schedule — day 43 is a patching day built around today's error log, not a
repeat mock. If your total is below 60, still log it and still do day 43's patching, but
do not move straight into Phase 4's mock cadence (days 44, 46, 48) until the gap is
closed — insert one or two extra output days repeating whichever module scored lowest,
using this same second Übungssatz, before treating yourself as ready for day 44. Slower
here beats booking the real exam under a false 60.

## Exercises

1. Score your Hören module out of its total and record the number, not just pass or fail. — **Hint:** the second Übungssatz's own answer key gives a points-out-of total — use that number, not a rounded impression. — **Solution sketch:** write `Hören: 18/25` in `progress_tracker.md`, with the specific number, not just a checkmark.
2. Sum all four module scores into one overall result against the 60+ gate. — **Hint:** Start Deutsch 1 is not modular: sum the four module scores out of 25 each into one total out of 100 and compare that total to 60. There is no separate written score, no separate Sprechen pass, and no module you can fail on its own. — **Solution sketch:** `18 + 16 + 15 + 14 = 63/100 — gate cleared.`
3. Write one sentence naming today's weakest module and why. — **Hint:** name the module and one concrete cause, not a vague "I need to study more." — **Solution sketch:** `Hören war heute schwach, weil ich die Zahlen nicht verstanden habe.`

## Anti-patterns / Common mistakes

- Adding new Tier 2 cards "just to not waste the day." Day 42 is a full mock and must
  never introduce new material — anything new today contaminates the score as a measure
  of what is actually installed.
- Talking yourself into a 30-minute italki call today because sessions are usually good.
  Today's Block 3 is explicitly rest, so the Sprechen module you record in Block 4
  reflects your normal voice, not a warmed-up one you won't have on the real exam morning
  either.

## Minimum viable day

None — the mock only produces a usable score if run in full. If you truly cannot find
the time today, do Block 1 only and move the entire mock to your next full session; never
take a partial mock and record it as day 42's result. See `runbook.md` §Bad days.
