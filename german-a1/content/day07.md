# Day 07 — W-questions: Wie, Wo, Woher, Was, Wann

**Phase:** 1 — Engine | **Total:** 2h15 | **New cards:** 25

## Why this matters

Sprechen Teil 2 is built entirely from questions and answers on a keyword card, and
every one of the fourteen theme questions in `templates/sprechen_scripts.md` starts with
a W-word. Today you fix the five most common ones — `Wie, Wo, Woher, Was, Wann` — as a
reflex, so that in Teil 2 you spend your thinking time on the answer, not on remembering
how to build the question.

## Core concepts

A [W-Frage](GLOSSARY.md#grammar) puts the question word first, the verb second, and
everything else after — the same "verb in position two" order every German main clause
uses. Five you need now:

```
Wie heißt du?             (how / what — asks for a name or manner)
Wo wohnst du?             (where — asks for a location)
Woher kommst du?          (where from — asks for origin)
Was machst du?            (what — asks for an action or thing)
Wann hast du Geburtstag?  (when — asks for a time)
```

Notice the verb always sits in the exact same slot, second, regardless of which W-word
starts the sentence — that fixed slot is what makes the pattern a drillable reflex
rather than five separate sentences to memorise.

## Exercises

1. Say all five W-question frames aloud with the verb correctly in second position. — **Hint:** the trap is putting the verb third, the way a translated English question order might tempt you (`Wo du wohnst?`) — the verb must sit immediately after the W-word. — **Solution sketch:** `Wie heißt du? Wo wohnst du? Woher kommst du? Was machst du? Wann hast du Geburtstag?`
2. Ask a W-question about a family member and answer it using a regular verb from day 6. — **Hint:** the answer's verb also needs the correct ending — a right question with a wrong answer ending is still an incomplete Teil 2 turn. — **Solution sketch:** `Wo wohnt deine Schwester? — Meine Schwester wohnt in Hue.`
3. From today's new words (theme 03_time, words 101–125), pick five nouns and produce article plus plural from memory. — **Hint:** if two of the five come back instantly and three don't, that's exactly the split Anki tracks — trust the app's rating over your own sense of "I basically know this." — **Solution sketch:** e.g. `der Montag, die Montage; der Januar, die Januare; der März, die Märze` — say each pair aloud twice, then check the wordlist.

## Anti-patterns / Common mistakes

- Putting the verb in third position by following English or Vietnamese word order under time pressure — `Wo du wohnst?` instead of `Wo wohnst du?`. Under exam stress this reverts first, so drill it now while calm.
- Answering a W-question with a single word instead of a full sentence — saying just `"Hanoi"` instead of `"Ich wohne in Hanoi."` Teil 2 is scored on sentence structure, not just correct content.
