# Day 39 — Opinions and unscripted small talk

**Phase:** 3 — Output | **Total:** 2h15 | **New cards:** 32

## Why this matters

Every session so far has had a shape you could prepare for. Today's italki session
deliberately does not — the tutor asks whatever comes up, the way the real exam's
examiner sometimes deviates from the card in front of them.

## Core concepts

Stating an opinion in German is a fixed sentence opener (`Ich finde, dass …`, `Meiner
Meinung nach …`) attached to whatever the moment throws at you — the opener is the part
you drilled; the content is improvised.

### Block 1 — Anki (25 min)

Clear both decks' review queues. Take today's 32 new cards as the next contiguous slice:
words 225–256, all of them inside `t2_smalltalk` (`und dir`, `bis bald`, `freut mich`,
`kein Problem`, `alles klar`, `das stimmt`, `wie bitte`, `ich verstehe nicht`,
`es tut mir leid`). The opinion openers this call is built on came in on days 37 and 38 —
they are review now, not new cards.

### Block 2 — Input (40 min)

Draft the German agenda message for today's unscripted call:

```
Hallo! Für Start Deutsch 1 möchte ich ohne Skript sprechen — bitte stellen Sie mir
spontan Fragen zum Alltag, zum Wetter, zu Hobbys oder zur Arbeit, wie im echten Leben. Ich
versuche, meine Meinung zu sagen, z. B. mit "Ich finde, dass …" oder "Meiner Meinung
nach …". Bitte korrigieren Sie mich erst am Ende. Die Sitzung dauert 30 Minuten. Danke!
```

Then run through your `t2_opinion` cards from days 37 and 38 from memory once, out loud,
each with a different real-life example.

### Block 3 — Output (40 min)

Book the 30-minute session on https://www.italki.com. Let the tutor lead with unscripted
questions. Answer every question with a full opinion sentence, not a single word.

### Block 4 — Exam drill (30 min)

Run all three Sprechen Teile from the second Übungssatz timed, back to back, from memory
— your second full timed Sprechen rehearsal after day 35's.

## Exercises

1. Write the agenda message requesting unscripted small talk. — **Hint:** ask explicitly for spontaneous questions and for corrections at the end, or the tutor may default to a scripted lesson. — **Solution sketch:** the agenda text above, sent before the call.
2. State an opinion about today's weather using `Ich finde, dass …`. — **Hint:** `dass` sends the verb to the end of its clause, same as `weil` from day 26. — **Solution sketch:** `Ich finde, dass das Wetter heute schön ist.`
3. After the call, write one spontaneous question your tutor asked that you did not expect, plus your answer. — **Hint:** write both down immediately — an unexpected question is exactly the kind that resurfaces on exam day. — **Solution sketch:** e.g. `Was machen Sie am Wochenende?` answered `Am Wochenende bleibe ich zu Hause, weil ich müde bin.`

## Anti-patterns / Common mistakes

- Falling back on your memorised Teil 1 script the moment the conversation goes
  off-script. Unscripted small talk exists specifically to test what happens when nothing
  is memorised.
- Answering every opinion question with a single word (`Ja`, `Gut`) instead of a full
  opinion sentence — at A1 you are scored on producing a sentence, not just a correct
  fact.

## Minimum viable day

Block 1 + Block 3 only (45 min) — the call is today's Output block, so keep it if it is
booked. If you cannot take the call at all, do Block 1, then answer five self-set
questions out loud with a full opinion sentence each as your Output, and move the call to
your next full session. See `runbook.md` §Bad days.
