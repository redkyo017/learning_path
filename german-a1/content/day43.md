# Day 43 — Patching the day-42 error log; Hören at half speed

**Phase:** 4 — Simulation | **Total:** 2h15 | **New cards:** 0

## Why this matters

Phase 4 alternates four timed mocks with four patching days, and today is the first
patching day. You add no new vocabulary and touch no new grammar from here to day 50 —
Tier 1 and Tier 2 are both complete, and every Anki deck is now review-only. The only job
left is closing the gap between your day-42 score and 100: working the exact items
day-42's mock proved you get wrong, starting with Hören, the module where a single
mishearing costs a mark you cannot get back by knowing the word.

## Core concepts

Your day-42 mock produced an error log: for every item you missed, the module, the item,
what you answered, what the correct answer was, and — once you look closer — why you
missed it. Keep this on paper or in a private note, the same way `templates/sprechen_scripts.md`
keeps your personal details out of this repo. Today you work that log end to end, then
re-drill every missed Hören item specifically, at half speed, because a mishearing you
can't catch at full speed is often audible the moment the same audio is slowed down — the
fix is proving you can then catch it back at full speed too.

### Block 1 — Anki (25 min)

Review only. New cards/day is 0 from today through day 50 for both `tier1_wortliste.csv`
and `tier2_fluency.csv` — clear whatever backlog is due and rate honestly. There is
nothing new to learn in Anki for the rest of this path, only reviews to protect.

### Block 2 — Input (40 min)

Go through the day-42 error log module by module — Hören, Lesen, Schreiben, Sprechen. For
every missed item, write down the likely cause next to your wrong answer: a mishearing,
the wrong article, missed word order, a missed keyword, or running out of time. Do not
fix anything yet — just diagnose.

### Block 3 — Output (40 min)

Open the Übungssatz audio you already have from https://www.goethe.de/en/spr/kup/prf/prf/sd1/ueb.html
and slow it to half speed using your player's speed control. Replay every Hören item your
error log flags as missed, transcribe what you hear this time, and check the
transcription against the script or answer key.

### Block 4 — Exam drill (30 min)

Replay the same missed Hören items again, this time at full, normal speed, timed exactly
as the real module runs. If you now answer correctly without the half-speed crutch, the
item is fixed. If you still miss it, add a note to the log — that item goes back into
rotation before day 44.

## Exercises

1. A common day-42 Hören error: you write "Der Rock kostet siebzehn Euro" for audio that said "siebzig Euro." — **Hint:** both words have two syllables; they differ only in the last one — `-zehn` with a long *e* and a final *n*, `-zig` with a short *i* and a soft *ch* ending. At normal speed that difference is easy to lose. — **Solution sketch:** `Der Rock kostet siebzig Euro.` Replay at half speed, confirm you hear the short *i* and the soft *ch* of `-zig` rather than the long *e* of `-zehn`, then say both numbers side by side five times.
2. A common day-42 Hören error: you circle "Miete" where the correct answer was "Mitte." — **Hint:** the two words differ only in the vowel — `Miete` has a long *ie*, `Mitte` a short *i* before a double *t* — and a long/short vowel contrast is exactly the kind Vietnamese does not use to tell words apart. — **Solution sketch:** `Die Wohnung ist in der Stadtmitte.` Replay the item at half speed twice, then say `Miete` and `Mitte` side by side five times before moving on.
3. A common day-42 Hören error: you write "im Juni" for a date the speaker said as "im Juli." — **Hint:** the two month names differ by a single vowel and are easy to swap under time pressure. — **Solution sketch:** `Der Termin ist im Juli.` Replay at half speed, listen for the final vowel, then say the corrected sentence aloud three times.

## Anti-patterns / Common mistakes

- Using today's Block 2 or 3 to learn Hören vocabulary you didn't recognise, instead of fixing why you misheard words you already know. Almost every word in the mock is already in Tier 1 or Tier 2 — a fresh miss is almost always a hearing problem, not a knowledge gap, and treating it as the latter wastes the one session built to fix the former.
- Listening to a missed item once at half speed and marking it fixed. Half speed only exposes the error; Block 4's full-speed replay is what proves the fix held. Skip it and you carry the same miss into day 44 without knowing it's still there.
