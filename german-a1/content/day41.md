# Day 41 — Exam phrases and the full simulation

**Phase:** 3 — Output | **Total:** 2h15 | **New cards:** 32

## Why this matters

This is the last chance to fail safely, with a tutor correcting you, before day 42's mock
actually counts toward the 60+ gate. Today's new cards are survival phrases for the one
moment every candidate hits: not understanding the question.

## Core concepts

Silence costs more marks than a clarifying question. `t2_examphrases` exist so that not
understanding something never means going quiet.

### Block 1 — Anki (25 min)

Clear both decks' review queues. Take today's 32 new cards as the last contiguous slice:
words 289–320, all of them inside `t2_examphrases` (`können Sie mir helfen`,
`ich brauche Hilfe`, `das habe ich nicht verstanden`, `können Sie das bitte erklären`,
`ich weiß die Antwort nicht`, `ich bin fertig`, `ich übe noch`). Word 320 is the last row
in the file, so this closes out the Tier 2 deck — all 320 words are now introduced and
day 42 adds no more new cards. The clarifying phrases in today's agenda message
(`Können Sie das bitte wiederholen?`, `Können Sie bitte langsamer sprechen?`) came in
yesterday, on day 40.

### Block 2 — Input (40 min)

Reread `templates/sprechen_scripts.md` in full, all three Teile, once. Then draft the
German agenda message for today's full simulation:

```
Hallo! Ich möchte heute die Prüfung Start Deutsch 1 simulieren — Sprechen, Teil 1, 2 und
3: Teil 1 (Vorstellung), Teil 2 (Fragen zu einem Stichwort) und Teil 3 (eine Bitte und
eine Reaktion). Bitte spielen Sie die Prüferin oder den Prüfer und unterbrechen Sie mich
nicht. Falls ich etwas nicht verstehe, sage ich: "Können Sie das bitte
wiederholen?" oder "Können Sie bitte langsamer sprechen?". Feedback gern erst am Ende.
Die Sitzung dauert 30 Minuten. Danke!
```

### Block 3 — Output (40 min)

Book the 30-minute session on https://www.italki.com. Run all three Sprechen Teile back
to back with the tutor playing examiner. Use a `t2_examphrases` line live at least once if
you genuinely don't understand something — don't just push through in silence.

### Block 4 — Exam drill (30 min)

Run the full Schreiben module from the second Übungssatz
(https://www.goethe.de/en/spr/kup/prf/prf/sd1/ueb.html) under real timing — your last
graded writing rehearsal before day 42.

## Exercises

1. Write the agenda message for the full Sprechen simulation. — **Hint:** ask the tutor to hold all interruptions and feedback until the end, matching real exam conditions. — **Solution sketch:** the agenda text above, sent before the call.
2. Use one `t2_examphrase` live during the simulation when you don't understand a question, and write down which one. — **Hint:** pick the phrase before the call starts, so it's ready the instant you need it, not composed on the spot. — **Solution sketch:** `Können Sie das bitte wiederholen?`
3. Rate your Teil 1, Teil 2 and Teil 3 from today's simulation on a 1–3 scale and name the weakest. — **Hint:** score honestly right after the call, while the stumbles are still fresh, not the next morning. — **Solution sketch:** e.g. `Teil 1: 3, Teil 2: 2, Teil 3: 2 — Teil 3 ist schwach.`

## Anti-patterns / Common mistakes

- Staying silent when you don't understand a question in the simulation instead of using
  a `t2_examphrase`. Silence costs more marks than a well-formed clarifying question.
- Skipping today's simulation because "day 42 is the real mock anyway." Day 41 is the
  last chance to fail safely, with a tutor correcting you, before day 42's score actually
  counts.

## Minimum viable day

Block 1 + Block 3 only (45 min) — the call is today's Output block, so keep it if it is
booked. If you cannot take the call at all, do Block 1, then run all three Sprechen Teile
solo and recorded as your Output, and move the call to your next full session. See
`runbook.md` §Bad days.
