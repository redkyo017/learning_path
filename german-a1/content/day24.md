# Day 24 — Dativ as fixed chunks

**Phase:** 2 — Expansion | **Total:** 2h15 | **New cards:** 25

## Why this matters

You now meet [Dativ](GLOSSARY.md#grammar) — but only as whole, memorised phrases, never
as a declension table. `mit dem Bus`, `zum Arzt` and similar chunks appear constantly in
Hören directions and Lesen notices, and recognising them as single units is faster and
more reliable than trying to derive them from a rule you were never taught. From today,
Block 4 also moves to full Übungssatz sections instead of single tasks.

## Core concepts

Treat each of these as one inseparable unit, the same way you memorise a noun with its
article — do not try to break them into "rules" about der/die/das changing.

- `Ich gehe zum Arzt.` (I'm going to the doctor.) — today's first chunk, learned whole.
- `Ich fahre mit dem Bus zum Geschäft.` (I'm going to the shop by bus.) — `mit dem Bus`
  is the exact chunk `STRATEGY.md` uses to show what "a chunk, not a four-by-four grid of
  endings" means in practice.
- `Ich fahre mit der Fähre.` (I'm going by ferry.)
- `Die Jacke ist in der Umkleidekabine.` (The jacket is in the fitting room.)

### Block 1 — Anki (25 min)

Clear the review queue first. Then study today's 25 new cards: theme 12_weather, words
526–550. Read the German, article and plural on the front, check the Vietnamese gloss and
example sentence on the back, and rate honestly.

### Block 2 — Input (40 min)

Open Nicos Weg: https://learngerman.dw.com/en/nicos-weg/c-36519789
Watch the episodes on getting around by bus and train and on shopping for clothes.
Watch each twice: once for gist, once shadowing aloud, writing down every `mit dem`,
`zum` or `zur` phrase you hear whole.

### Block 3 — Output (40 min)

Write, then audit. Using `templates/schreiben_templates.md`, Teil 2, write a friend a
message saying you're going to the shop by bus, asking whether they want to come, and
suggesting three o'clock — with `mit dem Bus` and `zum Geschäft` both in it. Take 10
minutes. Then do the part that matters today: go back through your own text, circle every
Dativ phrase, and compare each one letter for letter against the four chunks in Core
concepts. A chunk you altered while writing (`mit der Bus`) is exactly the error this
day's grammar exists to prevent, and you will only see it on the second pass.

### Block 4 — Exam drill (30 min)

Open the Übungssatz: https://www.goethe.de/en/spr/kup/prf/prf/sd1/ueb.html
Work the full Hören module (all three parts), timed as the paper instructs. Check every
answer against the key and note which part cost you the most time.

## Exercises

1. Say "I'm going to the doctor" using the fixed chunk `zum Arzt`. — **Hint:** memorise `zum Arzt` as one unit — do not try to build it from `zu` + `der Arzt` by rule. — **Solution sketch:** `Ich gehe zum Arzt.` Check you wrote `zum`, not `zu der` or `zu den`.
2. Say "I'm going by ferry" using `mit der Fähre`. — **Hint:** `mit der Fähre` is another whole chunk to memorise, not derived from a table — it is the feminine partner to `mit dem Bus`. — **Solution sketch:** `Ich fahre mit der Fähre.` Confirm you wrote `mit der`, not `mit die`.
3. Say "I'm going to the shop by bus," combining two Dativ chunks in one sentence. — **Hint:** `mit dem Bus` and `zum Geschäft` can both appear in the same sentence, each memorised separately. — **Solution sketch:** `Ich fahre mit dem Bus zum Geschäft.` Check both chunks are intact and unchanged.

## Anti-patterns / Common mistakes

- Trying to derive `zum` and `mit dem` from a case rule instead of memorising them as one fixed unit — this path deliberately skips the declension table, so treat `zum Arzt` as a single vocabulary item, not two words to analyse.
- Saying `mit der Bus` instead of `mit dem Bus` — pattern-matching from an unrelated feminine noun instead of memorising this exact phrase.
