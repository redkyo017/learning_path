# Day 25 — Prepositions of place; Schreiben Teil 1

**Phase:** 2 — Expansion | **Total:** 2h15 | **New cards:** 25

## Why this matters

Today adds a second family of fixed location chunks — `unter dem Bett`, `neben der
Tür` — used constantly in Lesen notices and Hören directions describing where something
is. It also opens Schreiben Teil 1, the form-filling task, which you have not touched
since day 1's exam overview. Teil 1 has exact field labels and three traps that cost
marks even from candidates whose German is otherwise fine — you learn all of it today.

## Core concepts

Like yesterday's Dativ chunks, these are memorised as whole location phrases, not built
from a rule:

- `Die Tasche liegt auf dem Tisch.` (The bag is lying on the table.)
- `Der Mantel hängt neben der Tür.` (The coat is hanging next to the door.)
- `Die Schuhe stehen unter dem Bett.` (The shoes are under the bed.)
- `Die Kasse ist vor dem Geschäft.` (The till is in front of the shop.)

Now open `templates/schreiben_templates.md` and read Teil 1 — Formular ausfüllen in full:
the ten German field labels and, critically, the three traps — date order (TT.MM.JJJJ),
Postleitzahl before Wohnort, and a handwritten Unterschrift.

## Exercises

1. Say "The coat is hanging next to the door" using `neben der Tür`. — **Hint:** memorise `neben der Tür` as a fixed location phrase, the same way you memorised `mit dem Bus`. — **Solution sketch:** `Der Mantel hängt neben der Tür.` Check `neben der Tür` is intact.
2. Fill in a practice Geburtsdatum field with 3 November 1998, then check the field order. — **Hint:** German dates are day, then month, then year — trap 1 in `templates/schreiben_templates.md`. — **Solution sketch:** write `03.11.1998` in the Geburtsdatum field; check you did not write `11.03.1998`.
3. Fill in a practice address line with postal code 10115 and town Berlin, then check the order. — **Hint:** Postleitzahl comes before Wohnort on a German form — trap 2 in `templates/schreiben_templates.md`. — **Solution sketch:** write `10115 Berlin`, not `Berlin 10115`; check the digits come first.

## Anti-patterns / Common mistakes

- Writing the Geburtsdatum in month-first order (the English habit) instead of TT.MM.JJJJ — trap 1 from `templates/schreiben_templates.md`, and the single most common Teil 1 error.
- Typing or printing your name in the Unterschrift field instead of leaving it for a handwritten signature — trap 3 from the same file.
