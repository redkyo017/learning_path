# Day 23 — dürfen and sollen

**Phase:** 2 — Expansion | **Total:** 2h15 | **New cards:** 25

## Why this matters

`dürfen` (permission) and `sollen` (someone else's expectation) close out the six-modal
set the exam expects at A1. Confusing them with `können` and `wollen` is a common,
scoreable error — a sign notice that reads "Hier darf man nicht rauchen" is a Lesen staple,
and misreading `darf` as ability instead of permission changes the meaning of the whole
sentence.

## Core concepts

`dürfen` says something is allowed (or not); `sollen` says someone else expects or
instructed you to do something — neither is your own ability (`können`) or your own wish
(`wollen`/`möchte`).

- `Ich darf hier nicht rauchen.` (I'm not allowed to smoke here.)
- `Hier darf man nicht rauchen.` (Smoking is not allowed here.)
- `Du sollst früh ins Bett gehen.` (You should go to bed early.)
- `Darf ich das Fenster öffnen?` (May I open the window?)

Like `können`, `dürfen` swaps its stem vowel in the `ich`-form: `dürfen → ich darf`, not
`ich dürfe`. It is a vowel change (`ü` → `a`), not the two dots simply falling off —
memorise the infinitive and its `ich`-form as one pair.

## Exercises

1. Say "I am not allowed to smoke here" using `dürfen`. — **Hint:** `dürfen` expresses permission, and its `ich`-form swaps the stem vowel: `ich darf`, not `ich dürfe`. — **Solution sketch:** `Ich darf hier nicht rauchen.` Check `rauchen` is last and `nicht` sits directly before it.
2. Say "You should go to bed early" using `sollen`. — **Hint:** `sollen` expresses someone else's expectation, not your own wish. — **Solution sketch:** `Du sollst früh ins Bett gehen.` Confirm `gehen` is the last word.
3. Ask "May I open the window?" using `dürfen`. — **Hint:** in a question, the modal moves to position 1, before the subject. — **Solution sketch:** `Darf ich das Fenster öffnen?` Check `öffnen` sits at the very end.

## Anti-patterns / Common mistakes

- Confusing `dürfen` (permission) with `können` (ability) — saying `Ich kann hier nicht rauchen` when the meaning is "it is not allowed," which needs `Ich darf hier nicht rauchen`.
- Using `will` where the sentence means "I am supposed to" — that meaning needs `sollen`, e.g. `Ich soll pünktlich sein`, not `Ich will pünktlich sein`.
