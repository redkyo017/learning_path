# Day 49 — Patching the day-48 error log; exam-day logistics

**Phase:** 4 — Simulation | **Total:** 2h15 | **New cards:** 0

## Why this matters

No new German material today — the same rule as every patching day this phase. You close
out the day-48 error log using whichever method fits its errors best, and you spend the
rest of the day on the one thing left that is not German at all: the logistics of the exam
day itself. By day 50 you need the booking decision, not a scramble to work out where your
Prüfungszentrum is or what you're allowed to carry into the room.

## Core concepts

Keep using the same error-log format as every patching day this phase. Today's specific
fix is administrative, not linguistic: confirm your ID document, your Prüfungszentrum,
your arrival time, and what to bring and leave behind — once, in writing, so none of it is
a source of anxiety on the morning of day 50's real-world equivalent.

### Block 1 — Anki (25 min)

Review only, 0 new cards. Clear the backlog.

### Block 2 — Input (40 min)

Go through the day-48 error log module by module and write the likely cause next to each
miss, exactly as on day 43, 45 and 47.

### Block 3 — Output (40 min)

Fix the day-48 log using whichever method carried the most misses: if Hören, re-drill the
missed items at half speed as on day 43; if Schreiben, rewrite the failed answers as on
day 45; if Sprechen, re-record the stalled answers as on day 47. No new technique — reuse
the one that already worked.

### Block 4 — Exam drill (30 min)

Read the official exam page: https://www.goethe.de/en/spr/kup/prf/prf/sd1.html
Confirm, in writing (on paper or in a private note, not here): your ID document (a valid
passport, or whichever photo ID your specific Prüfungszentrum's registration confirmation
names), your booked centre's address, and your planned arrival time — aim to arrive 30 to
45 minutes before the printed start time, since the Hören module cannot be paused or
replayed for a late arrival. Note what to bring — your ID, your registration
confirmation, and plain blue or black pens — and what is not allowed in the room: phones
and smartwatches switched off and stored away, no dictionaries, no notes. Confirm the
exact local list against your own centre's confirmation email, since small details vary
by centre.

## Exercises

1. A common day-48 error: you write "die Tisch" instead of the correct article in a Schreiben Teil 2 message. — **Hint:** `STRATEGY.md`'s Articles interference pattern — Vietnamese has no grammatical gender to guess from, so the article has to come back with the noun as one unit. — **Solution sketch:** `Der Tisch ist neu.` Say the corrected sentence aloud three times, article first.
2. A common day-48 error: you write "Ich fahren jeden Tag mit dem Bus" in a Schreiben answer. — **Hint:** Vietnamese verbs never inflect for person, so the ending has to be a rep, not a guess. — **Solution sketch:** `Ich fahre jeden Tag mit dem Bus.` Say it aloud, then write it once by hand.
3. A common day-48 error: your Sprechen recording shows a yes/no question said with falling pitch. — **Hint:** `STRATEGY.md`'s intonation section — a yes/no question is the one contour that rises. — **Solution sketch:** `Kommst du aus Köln?` said with a rising pitch at the end; mark the rise on the recording and re-record until it's audible.

## Anti-patterns / Common mistakes

- Leaving centre, ID and arrival-time logistics for the days after day 50 "once the score is in." Confirming them today, three mocks before the decision, removes exactly the kind of last-minute anxiety this phase exists to eliminate, regardless of what day 50's score turns out to be.
- Treating today's error-log work as optional because "logistics matters more today." The day-48 log still needs fixing — logistics is additional work this day, not a replacement for it.
