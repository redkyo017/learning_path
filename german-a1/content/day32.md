# Day 32 — Tier 2 opens: connectors

**Phase:** 3 — Output | **Total:** 2h15 | **New cards:** 32

## Why this matters

All 650 Tier 1 words are installed — from today the Tier 1 deck is review-only, forever.
What you add now are the words that turn a string of short sentences into something that
sounds like one connected thought: `deshalb`, `trotzdem`, `außerdem`, `zunächst`, `dann`. You
also run your first fully timed Hören module from the second Übungssatz — no more
30-minute tastes of the exam, the real clock.

## Core concepts

From today, Block 4 stops being a preview and becomes genuine exam timing. Treat every
Block 4 from here to day 50 as if a real invigilator were in the room.

### Block 1 — Anki (25 min)

Clear the Tier 1 review queue first — it is review-only from today onward, permanently 0
new cards; never add new Tier 1 cards again. Then create a new Anki deck named
`A1 Tier 2`. Import `vocabulary/tier2_fluency.csv`, same column mapping as Tier 1: german,
article, plural, english, vietnamese, example, note. Set new cards/day to 32. Take today's
32 new cards as a contiguous slice of the deck: words 1–32, all of them in the
`t2_connectors` group — `und`, `aber`, `deshalb`, `trotzdem`, `dann`, `danach`,
`zunächst`, `außerdem`, `zum Beispiel`. Take the rows in file order and do not skip
ahead; each of the ten Tier 2 days is the next 32 rows down the file.

### Block 2 — Input (40 min)

Open `vocabulary/wordlists/08_travel.md` and read it through once silently, once aloud —
you are refreshing the theme you speak about in Block 3, not learning it fresh. Then
reopen https://learngerman.dw.com/en/nicos-weg/c-36519789 and re-shadow whichever
travel-themed episode you first shadowed in Phase 1, this time at full native speed, no
subtitles, no pausing to replay a line.

### Block 3 — Output (40 min)

Speak a 90-second monologue on `08_travel`: where you have been, where you want to go,
how you get there. Use at least four `t2_connectors` words to link your sentences instead
of stringing bare clauses together with only `und`. Draft it once on paper, then deliver
and record it live without reading from the page. Play it back once. Do not correct it
yet — day 34 is for that.

### Block 4 — Exam drill (30 min)

Download the second Übungssatz from
https://www.goethe.de/en/spr/kup/prf/prf/sd1/ueb.html (same page as day 1's, a different
set) and run the full Hören module under real exam timing: play each audio track the
number of times the paper says, no pausing, no rewinding, mark your answer sheet as you
go. Score it against the answer key only after the timer stops.

## Exercises

1. Join these two ideas with `deshalb`: you have no time; you are not travelling to Germany this year. — **Hint:** `deshalb` sits in position 1 and pushes the verb into position 2, exactly like any other connector that opens the sentence. — **Solution sketch:** `Ich habe keine Zeit. Deshalb fahre ich dieses Jahr nicht nach Deutschland.`
2. Count how many `t2_connectors` words you actually used in your recorded monologue. — **Hint:** `und` is in today's slice but only glues clauses together — it does not structure them, so do not count it; you need `deshalb`, `trotzdem`, `außerdem`, `zunächst`, `dann` or `danach`. — **Solution sketch:** if you find only one, insert a second — e.g. `Zunächst fahre ich mit dem Zug. Danach fliege ich nach Berlin.`
3. Time your recording against the 90-second target. — **Hint:** first attempts usually land under 60 seconds — you are not short on content, you are pausing more than it feels like. — **Solution sketch:** add one more connector-linked sentence, e.g. `Ich fahre gern nach Österreich. Außerdem fahre ich gern in die Schweiz.`, and re-record.

## Anti-patterns / Common mistakes

- Adding `t2_connectors` to Anki and never once producing them in Block 3. A connector you
  can recognise on a flashcard but never say out loud has not moved your Sprechen score at
  all.
- Running the Hören module with pauses "just this once" because it is the first fully
  timed one. The exam plays each track a fixed number of times — twice in Teil 1 and
  Teil 3, once in Teil 2 — and never lets you pause or rewind, so practising with pauses
  trains a habit the real exam will not let you use.
