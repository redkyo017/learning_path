# Day 30 — Consolidation and mock preparation

**Phase:** 2 — Expansion | **Total:** 2h15 | **New cards:** 0

## Why this matters

Tomorrow is your first full mock — all four modules, one sitting, timed. Today has no new
grammar or vocabulary; its only job is to make sure nothing except your German knowledge
fails tomorrow: the paper is printed, the timer works, and your weakest words get one more
pass before the clock starts.

## Core concepts

No new grammar reflex today. Instead, review the six patterns from this phase in order —
haben-Perfekt, sein-Perfekt, irregular Perfekt, the six modals, Dativ chunks, weil/dass —
and for each one, say one example sentence aloud from memory before moving to the next.
If you stall on one, that pattern gets extra attention in Phase 3, not today.

### Block 1 — Anki (25 min)

Clear the review queue only — no new cards. This is the last pure-review day before the
mock; keep it a full 25 minutes even though the queue may feel light.

### Block 2 — Input (40 min)

Open Nicos Weg: https://learngerman.dw.com/en/nicos-weg/c-36519789
Skim back through the second half of the course at your own pace — no full rewatch — and
rewatch only the one episode that felt least clear the first time, shadowing it once.

### Block 3 — Output (40 min)

A dress rehearsal, not a practice message: closed book. Shut
`templates/schreiben_templates.md` and sit down with blank paper and the timer you will
use tomorrow. Ten minutes: write a friend a message about your exam preparation,
mentioning you practised a full Übungssatz today, asking whether they have practised too,
and proposing studying together before the real exam. Only when the timer rings, open the
template and mark your own message against the skeleton and the three-points rule.
Whatever you had to look up afterwards is what you memorise tonight, because tomorrow the
template is shut for the whole module.

### Block 4 — Exam drill (30 min)

Open the Übungssatz page: https://www.goethe.de/en/spr/kup/prf/prf/sd1/ueb.html
Print (or save as a ready-to-print PDF) all four modules of one Übungssatz plus its audio
and answer key, laid out exactly as you will use them tomorrow. Then set the physical
timer you will use for the mock and run one 20-minute Hören section under it, stopping the
instant it rings, to confirm the setup works end to end.

## Exercises

1. Print (or save as PDF, ready to print) all four modules of one Übungssatz and check every page is present. — **Hint:** the exam page lists Hören, Lesen, Schreiben and Sprechen as separate sections — a missing page means an incomplete mock tomorrow. — **Solution sketch:** open https://www.goethe.de/en/spr/kup/prf/prf/sd1/ueb.html, download the Übungssatz PDF and its audio, and confirm you have four labelled sections plus the answer key before closing the tab.
2. Set a physical timer for 20 minutes and complete one Hören section under it, then check you stopped writing the instant it rang. — **Hint:** tomorrow's mock is scored partly on whether you finish in time — practising the stop-the-instant-it-rings habit today avoids losing time tomorrow. — **Solution sketch:** run the timer, work the section, and the moment it rings put your pen down — if you were still writing, that habit is what you fix today, not tomorrow.
3. Write out your five weakest words from the Anki lapse list by hand, with article and plural. — **Hint:** `runbook.md`'s weekly review step recommends handwriting your worst ten words on paper — do a smaller version of that here as final consolidation. — **Solution sketch:** e.g. `das Geschäft, die Geschäfte` — write each of your five words this way, article and plural together, not the noun alone.

## Anti-patterns / Common mistakes

- Skipping today's timer test and discovering tomorrow, mid-mock, that your phone timer alarm is silent or your printer is out of ink — test the exact setup you will use for the mock today, not tomorrow.
- Cramming new vocabulary today "just in case" — day 30 is consolidation, not expansion; new material this close to the mock crowds out review time and does not move tomorrow's score.
