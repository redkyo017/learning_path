Perfekt and modal sentence you built this phase.

## Core concepts

Fronting a time word or phrase does not push the verb to third position — the verb stays
second, and the subject moves after it instead.

- `Gestern bin ich in die Stadt gegangen.` (Yesterday I went into the city.)
- `Um sieben Uhr bin ich aufgestanden.` (At seven o'clock I got up.)
- `Am Montag habe ich einen Termin.` (On Monday I have an appointment.)

Read each of the 14 Antwort sentences in `templates/sprechen_scripts.md` Teil 2 and check
the verb's position in every one — all fourteen are verb-second. A leading `Ja,` or
`Danke,` does not count as the first element: in `Ja, ich habe einen Bruder.` the first
element is `ich` and the verb `habe` is still second.

## Anti-patterns / Common mistakes

- Putting the verb in third position after a fronted time expression, e.g. `Gestern ich bin gegangen` instead of `Gestern bin ich gegangen` — whatever comes first, the conjugated verb is always the second element, even when the subject is not first.
- Assuming verb-second is a Sprechen-only rule — it applies identically in Schreiben; a Schreiben Teil 2 message with the verb in the wrong slot loses marks even if every word is spelled correctly.
