# Day 38 — Schreiben Teil 2 against the clock

**Phase:** 3 — Output | **Total:** 2h15 | **New cards:** 32

## Why this matters

Ten minutes is tight for a formal message that must hit all three required points. Today
you write one under real time pressure, not the unlimited time you have used until now.

## Core concepts

Formal register (`Sehr geehrte …`, `Mit freundlichen Grüßen`) is a fixed skeleton, not a
style you compose fresh each time — the speed comes from reusing the skeleton, not from
writing faster.

### Block 1 — Anki (25 min)

Clear both decks' review queues. Take today's 32 new cards as the next contiguous slice:

Reread `templates/schreiben_templates.md`'s formal worked example and the Feld table with

### Block 3 — Output (40 min)

Write a new formal Teil 2 message under 10 minutes: you have a Termin beim Arzt (doctor's

30 words. Use at least one `t2_adverbs` word naturally. When the 10 minutes are up, check
the message against the three required points one by one, per `templates/schreiben_templates.md`'s rule.

### Block 4 — Exam drill (30 min)

Run the full Schreiben module from the second Übungssatz
(https://www.goethe.de/en/spr/kup/prf/prf/sd1/ueb.html) under real timing — both Teil 1
and Teil 2.

## Exercises

1. Write the formal opening and closing lines for a message to a Frau Schmidt. — **Hint:** the greeting and the closing are fixed phrases you memorise once, not phrases you compose per message. — **Solution sketch:** `Sehr geehrte Frau Schmidt,` … then, on two separate lines and with no comma after the closing formula: `Mit freundlichen Grüßen` / `[Vorname] [Nachname]`
2. Insert one `t2_adverbs` word into your Teil 2 message naturally. — **Hint:** `leider` (unfortunately) fits directly before the reason clause in a cancellation message. — **Solution sketch:** `Ich kann leider nicht kommen.`
3. Check your finished message against the prompt's three points. — **Hint:** a well-written message that misses one point still loses that point's marks — check points before you check grammar. — **Solution sketch:** list the three points (which appointment, reason and new day, request for confirmation) and confirm each is covered by name, e.g. `Punkt 3 fehlt → Können Sie mir das bitte kurz bestätigen?` added.

## Anti-patterns / Common mistakes

- Writing a grammatically clean message that only covers two of the three required
  points. Per `templates/schreiben_templates.md`'s rule, a missing point loses roughly a
  third of the Teil 2 marks regardless of grammar quality. Nothing is pass/fail on its
  own: Start Deutsch 1 is one exam scored out of 100, not four modules you can fail
  individually.
- Spending the full 10 minutes composing before checking word count or the three points —
  check both at minute 7, while there is still time left to fix a gap.
