# Day 37 — Transcribe and correct yourself, round two

**Phase:** 3 — Output | **Total:** 2h15 | **New cards:** 32

## Why this matters

A second transcription only earns its place if you check it against the first. Today
tells you whether day 34's three flagged errors are gone, shrinking, or still there.

## Core concepts

Correction that does not get re-measured is just a diary entry. Today's exercise is the
measurement, not a fresh, unrelated error hunt.

### Block 1 — Anki (25 min)

Clear both decks' review queues. Take today's 32 new cards as the next contiguous slice:
words 161–192. Rows 161–176 finish `t2_adverbs` (`nochmal`, `ungefähr`, `vielleicht`,
`hoffentlich`, `leider`, `natürlich`, `endlich`, `kaum`) and rows 177–192 open
`t2_opinion` (`ich finde`, `ich glaube`, `ich denke`, `meiner Meinung nach`, `lieber`,
`am liebsten`).

### Block 2 — Input (40 min)

Reopen https://learngerman.dw.com/en/nicos-weg/c-36519789 and re-shadow another episode
you have not yet re-shadowed this phase, full native speed, no subtitles. Keep this
separate from the transcription task in Block 3.

### Block 3 — Output (40 min)

Play your day-35 recording (`13_freetime`). Transcribe it word for word, the same method
as day 34. Then pull up day 34's list of three circled errors and check each one
specifically: did it recur in this new transcript, or not? Circle today's three worst
errors as well, whether or not they overlap with day 34's list.

### Block 4 — Exam drill (30 min)

Run the Lesen module from the second Übungssatz
(https://www.goethe.de/en/spr/kup/prf/prf/sd1/ueb.html) under full timing.

## Exercises

1. Compare today's transcript to day 34's three circled errors — did any recur? — **Hint:** check each one specifically by name, don't just eyeball the new transcript for a general impression. — **Solution sketch:** if `mit Zug` (missing article) reappears, write the corrected form again: `mit dem Zug`, and note it recurred.
2. Find one place your Teil 2 mock skipped a `t2_modals` form you had drilled. — **Hint:** look for a spot where you answered with a bare fact instead of building out `ich möchte` or `ich kann` into a full sentence. — **Solution sketch:** `Ich lese Bücher` expanded to `Ich lese gern Bücher, und ich möchte gern mehr lesen.`
3. Rewrite the day-35 monologue's weakest sentence correctly. — **Hint:** pick the sentence you stumbled on when replaying it, not the one that merely sounds plain. — **Solution sketch:** e.g. `Ich mache Sport am Wochenende` corrected to `Am Wochenende mache ich Sport.`

## Anti-patterns / Common mistakes

- Treating today as a repeat of day 34 instead of checking whether the same errors are
  recurring — the point of a second transcription is measuring whether correction
  actually stuck, not generating a fresh list every time.
- Skipping the comparison against day 34's error list because "it's basically the same
  drill." Without the comparison you cannot tell whether you are improving or just
  restarting from zero each time.
