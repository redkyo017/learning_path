# Day 48 — Full mock 5

**Phase:** 4 — Simulation | **Total:** 2h15 | **New cards:** 0

## Why this matters

Today is a full mock, Mock 5 of six, and the last one before day 49's exam-logistics day
and day 50's final mock and booking decision. It tells you whether yesterday's
re-recorded Sprechen answers hold cold, under real time pressure, with a keyword card you
have not seen before.

## Core concepts

Same non-modular scoring as every mock this phase: four modules out of 25 each, one
combined total out of 100, pass at 60, no module passable or failable alone.

### Block 1 — Anki (25 min)

Review only, 0 new cards. Clear the backlog before anything else.

### Block 2 — Input (40 min)

Open the next Übungssatz you have not sat yet, from
https://www.goethe.de/en/spr/kup/prf/prf/sd1/ueb.html — and if no unseen set is left,
re-sit one under the Übungssatz-supply rule in `runbook.md` §Mock papers and the Übungssatz supply. Run Hören, then Lesen,
back to back, each timed as printed on the paper. This is the fatigue sitting: run it at
the time of day your exam is booked for, or late in your day if you have not booked yet,
and expect the score to say as much about stamina as about knowledge.

### Block 3 — Output (40 min)

Run the Schreiben module timed, Teil 1 and Teil 2, using only the exam paper — cold, with
no warm-up at all: no rereading of `templates/schreiben_templates.md` first, no practice
message. Whatever the skeleton has become by now is what you will have on exam morning.

### Block 4 — Exam drill (30 min)

Run the Sprechen module timed and recorded, all three Teile, cold, with no rehearsal
beforehand. Score every module and log Hören/25, Lesen/25, Schreiben/25, Sprechen/25 and
the total /100 in `progress_tracker.md`'s Mock log as Mock 5 (day 48). Write the error
log immediately afterward, while the mistakes are still fresh, ready for day 49's
patching.

## Exercises

1. Before Schreiben Teil 2 starts, recite the word-count target and the three-points rule from memory. — **Hint:** `templates/schreiben_templates.md`'s own rule — a missing point loses marks regardless of grammar. — **Solution sketch:** about 30 words, all three prompt points covered; check every point off against the prompt before checking grammar.
2. Before recording, say one fresh Sprechen Teil 2 question-answer pair from memory, on any theme, with no rehearsal. — **Hint:** `templates/sprechen_scripts.md`'s Teil 2 keyword-card format — the exam always gives you a theme, never the sentence itself. — **Solution sketch:** e.g. `Was trinken Sie gern? / Ich trinke gern Tee.` Say both lines cold before the real Teil 2 begins.
3. Immediately after scoring, compare today's total to your last three mocks (day 42, day 44, day 46) before you do anything else. — **Hint:** four points make the trend clearer than three did on day 46. — **Solution sketch:** write all four totals in a row in `progress_tracker.md`'s Mock log — e.g. 58, 63, 67, 70 — and note the trend before starting day 49's error-log work.

## Anti-patterns / Common mistakes

- Delaying the error-log write-up because "you'll remember the mistakes later." The exact distractor that tricked you fades within a day, and day 49's whole session depends on that log being accurate, not reconstructed from memory.
- Reusing a rehearsed Sprechen answer from day 44 or day 46 instead of a fresh, cold recording today. A rehearsed answer hides whether fatigue or an unfamiliar keyword card would make you stall for real, which is exactly what day 50 needs to know.

## Minimum viable day

Same exception as day 44 and day 46: a full mock cannot be split. If you cannot run the
whole session, do Block 1 only (25 min) and move the entire mock, unstarted, to your next
session. See `runbook.md` §Bad days for the general rule this overrides on mock days.
