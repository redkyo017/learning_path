# Day 33 — italki session #1: Teil 1 and Teil 2 live

**Phase:** 3 — Output | **Total:** 2h15 | **New cards:** 32

## Why this matters

Every spoken word so far has gone to a recording, not a person. Today a real tutor answers
back, and Sprechen stops being a solo drill. A community tutor on italki costs roughly
$8–10/hr; a 30-minute session is about $4–5, and it is the single highest-leverage half
hour in this phase.

## Core concepts

An unplanned italki session drifts into general chat. A planned one drills exactly the
exam parts you tell the tutor to drill — that plan is written before the call, not
improvised during it.

### Block 1 — Anki (25 min)

Clear both decks' review queues. Take today's 32 new cards as the next contiguous slice
of `vocabulary/tier2_fluency.csv`: words 33–64. That slice crosses a group boundary —
rows 33–45 are the last of `t2_connectors` (`das heißt`, `anschließend`, `vor allem`,
`insgesamt`, `kurzum`, `immerhin`) and rows 46–64 open `t2_modals` (`ich kann`,
`du kannst`, `ich muss`, `ich will`, `ich darf`). Slices, not groups, set the daily
quota, so a day often spans two groups.

### Block 2 — Input (40 min)

Reread `templates/sprechen_scripts.md` in full: your filled-in Teil 1 script and the
Teil 2 theme table. Then draft the written agenda you will send your tutor before the
call, in German, naming the exact exam parts:

```
Hallo! Ich lerne für die Prüfung Start Deutsch 1 (A1) und möchte den Sprechen-Teil üben.
Können wir bitte Folgendes machen? 1) Ich stelle mich vor (Teil 1: Name, Alter, Land,
Wohnort, Sprache, Beruf, Hobby) — bitte korrigieren Sie mich danach kurz. 2) Wir üben
Teil 2: Sie geben mir ein Stichwort (z. B. Reisen, Essen, Wetter), ich stelle eine Frage,
Sie antworten, dann tauschen wir die Rollen. Die Sitzung dauert 30 Minuten. Vielen Dank!
```

Send it before the session, not at the start of it. Note that this agenda — and every
tutor agenda in this phase — is written above A1: it is a message you copy, fill in and
send, not German you are expected to compose yourself at day 33.

### Block 3 — Output (40 min)

Book a 30-minute community-tutor session on https://www.italki.com. Run your Teil 1
introduction from memory, then work through the Teil 2 theme table in both directions —
you ask, they answer; they ask, you answer. Ask the tutor to hold corrections until the
end, per your agenda.

### Block 4 — Exam drill (30 min)

Run the Lesen module from the second Übungssatz
(https://www.goethe.de/en/spr/kup/prf/prf/sd1/ueb.html) under full exam timing, no
dictionary, no pausing between parts. Score it against the answer key afterward.

## Exercises

1. Write your Teil 1 self-introduction from `templates/sprechen_scripts.md`, brackets filled with placeholders, and say it in under 30 seconds. — **Hint:** the seven-sentence script matches the exam's seven expected points — don't add an eighth. — **Solution sketch:** `Ich heiße [Vorname] [Nachname]. Ich bin [Alter] Jahre alt. Ich komme aus Vietnam. Ich wohne in [Stadt]. Ich spreche Vietnamesisch und ein bisschen Deutsch. Ich bin [Beruf] von Beruf. Mein Hobby ist [Hobby].`
2. Write the German agenda message you will send your tutor before the call. — **Hint:** name the exam parts explicitly — a message that just says "practise speaking" gets a vague session back. — **Solution sketch:** the agenda text above, sent as a private message on italki, not read aloud during the call.
3. After the call, write one sentence your tutor corrected, in its corrected form. — **Hint:** write down the fixed version immediately, before the session's memory fades. — **Solution sketch:** e.g. tutor corrects `Ich wohne Hanoi` to `Ich wohne in Hanoi.` — write that corrected sentence, not just the error.

## Anti-patterns / Common mistakes

- Booking the italki session but sending no agenda, leaving the tutor to freestyle small
  talk instead of drilling Teil 1 and Teil 2 format — you lose a paid session's worth of
  targeted practice.
- Treating the tutor's real-time correction as something to fix mid-sentence, which trains
  hesitation. Ask for corrections only at the end, exactly as your agenda message requests.

## Minimum viable day

Block 1 + Block 3 only (45 min) — the call is today's Output block, so if it is booked,
keep it. If you cannot take the call at all, do Block 1, then produce your Teil 1
introduction out loud and record it as your Output, and draft the agenda and book the call
for your next full session. See `runbook.md` §Bad days.
