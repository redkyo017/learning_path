# Day 31 — The first full mock

**Phase:** 2 — Expansion | **Total:** 2h15 | **New cards:** 0

## Why this matters

This is the first full mock of the path: all four modules — Hören, Lesen, Schreiben,
Sprechen — completed in one sitting, timed, exactly as the real Goethe-Zertifikat A1:
Start Deutsch 1 runs. It closes Phase 2 and sets the baseline every later mock in Phase 3
and Phase 4 is measured against. No new grammar or vocabulary appears today — you use only
what the last 30 days built.

## Core concepts

No new grammar reflex today. The four blocks keep their usual headings, but their content
changes: Block 1 is review only, and Blocks 2–4 are occupied by the mock itself rather
than input, output and drill practice. And today's real time split is not 25/40/40/30 —
the modules set the clock, not the blocks, and the mock plus its marking absorbs most of
the day's 2h15. Expect to run over; plan the day around finishing the mock, not around
the block timings.

### Block 1 — Anki (25 min)

Clear the review queue only — no new cards. Do this before the mock starts, not during a
break in the middle of it.

### Block 2 — Input (40 min)

This block is now Hören and Lesen. Open the Übungssatz you printed yesterday:
https://www.goethe.de/en/spr/kup/prf/prf/sd1/ueb.html
Work the full Hören module first, playing each audio track only as many times as the real
exam allows — twice in Teil 1 and Teil 3, once in Teil 2 — then the full Lesen module,
both under their official timings back to back. Those timings are roughly 20 and 25
minutes, so together they run about five minutes past this block; that is expected, and
the paper's clock wins over the block's. Do not check answers yet.

### Block 3 — Output (40 min)

This block is now Schreiben. Complete the full Schreiben module — Teil 1 (the form) and
Teil 2 (the short message) — under official timing, using `templates/schreiben_templates.md`
only to double-check the three Teil 1 traps and the three-points rule for Teil 2, not to
look up new content mid-mock. Do not check answers yet.

### Block 4 — Exam drill (30 min)

This block is now Sprechen plus scoring, and it is the block most likely to overrun —
Sprechen alone runs about 15 minutes, and marking four modules against the answer key
takes what it takes. Let it run past 30 minutes rather than rushing the marking. Record
yourself completing the full Sprechen module solo — Teil 1, Teil 2, Teil 3 — using `templates/sprechen_scripts.md` only as your
memorised baseline, not as a script to read live. Then mark all four modules against the
Übungssatz answer key and log all five numbers — Hören, Lesen, Schreiben, Sprechen and
Total — in `progress_tracker.md`'s Mock log, row "1 (day 31)".

## Exercises

1. Pull your Hören score and rewrite the one item you got wrong. — **Hint:** a wrong Hören answer is almost always a missed detail, not a missed word — replay just that clip once more and listen for a number, a time, or a place you skipped past. — **Solution sketch:** e.g. if you missed a departure time, write out the full sentence you now hear: `Der Zug fährt um acht Uhr fünfzehn ab.` Add it to your error log with the timestamp of the clip.
2. Pull one Schreiben Teil 2 sentence you wrote during the mock and check it against the three-point rule. — **Hint:** re-read the prompt's three points one at a time against your message — a grammatically perfect message that misses one point still loses that point's marks. Start Deutsch 1 is one exam scored out of 100, pass at 60; no module fails on its own. — **Solution sketch:** if you missed the reply-request point, add it: `Kannst du mir bitte kurz schreiben?` Rewrite the full message with all three points present.
3. Log your five mock numbers in `progress_tracker.md` and identify your weakest module. — **Hint:** the Mock log table takes Hören, Lesen, Schreiben, Sprechen and Total — five numbers, not four. — **Solution sketch:** fill in row "1 (day 31)" with your four module scores and their sum as Total, then circle the lowest of the four — that module gets extra Output and Exam-drill time in Phase 3.

## Anti-patterns / Common mistakes

- Stopping to look up a word mid-module during the mock — the real exam gives you no dictionary; if you don't know it, guess and move on, then add it to your error log afterward.
- Skipping the error log because the mock is "just practice" — the whole value of day 31 is the five numbers and the specific errors you log; a mock you don't log is a mock you can't learn from.

## Minimum viable day

There is no reduced version of a full mock — see `runbook.md` §Bad days for what to do if
you genuinely cannot run all four modules today: move the whole mock to your next
available full session rather than compressing it, since a partial mock produces numbers
you cannot compare against day 42's gate.
