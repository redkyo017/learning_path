# Day 11 — nicht and kein

**Phase:** 1 — Engine | **Total:** 2h15 | **New cards:** 25

## Why this matters

Today's 25 cards are all food words, but the home theme you finished yesterday is still
what you negate today: negation is the last piece missing before you can say
what your home does not have, not just what it does. The exam tests both negation words
directly, and Vietnamese has a single all-purpose negation particle, so this is a place
where L1 habit will pull you toward using only one German word where two are actually
needed.

## Core concepts

`nicht` negates a verb, an adjective, or one specific thing; `kein` (declined like
`ein`: `kein/keine/kein`) negates an indefinite noun — it replaces `ein`/`eine`/`ein`, or
negates a bare plural or uncountable noun.

```
Verb/adjective:  Ich wohne nicht in Berlin.   Das Zimmer ist nicht groß.
Indefinite noun: Ich habe keinen Balkon.  Ich habe keine Küche.  Ich habe kein Zimmer.
```

Notice `kein` follows the same masculine change you drilled yesterday — `keinen
Balkon`, not `kein Balkon` — because it's built on the same `ein`-pattern. The test that
decides which word you need: is there a noun with no article yet (or `ein`), or not? If
yes, `kein`. If the sentence has no noun to negate — just a verb or an adjective — use
`nicht`.

## Exercises

1. Negate three sentences, choosing `nicht` or `kein` correctly for each. — **Hint:** check first whether the thing being negated is a noun with `ein`/no article, or a verb/adjective with nothing to replace. — **Solution sketch:** `Ich habe einen Garten.` → `Ich habe keinen Garten.` / `Das Zimmer ist groß.` → `Das Zimmer ist nicht groß.` / `Ich wohne hier.` → `Ich wohne nicht hier.`
2. Say one `kein` sentence for each article — masculine, feminine, neuter — about something your home lacks. — **Hint:** `kein` changes exactly the way `ein` does — masculine `keinen`, feminine and neuter unchanged from `kein`/`keine`. — **Solution sketch:** `Ich habe keinen Garten. Ich habe keine Lampe. Ich habe kein Sofa.`
3. From today's new words (theme 05_food, words 201–225), pick five nouns and produce article plus plural from memory. — **Hint:** many food words are uncountable and carry no plural at all — `das Fleisch`, `der Käse`, `der Reis`, `das Salz` — so check what the card actually gives you instead of inventing a plural. — **Solution sketch:** e.g. `das Ei, die Eier; die Kartoffel, die Kartoffeln; der Kuchen, die Kuchen` — say each pair aloud twice, then check the wordlist.

## Anti-patterns / Common mistakes

- Using `nicht` to negate every kind of noun, the way Vietnamese uses one particle for everything — saying `Ich habe nicht einen Balkon` instead of `Ich habe keinen Balkon`. German splits this into two separate words on purpose; one particle for both roles doesn't exist.
- Forgetting to apply the masculine change to `kein` after correctly applying it to `ein` yesterday — saying `kein Balkon` instead of `keinen Balkon`. `kein` inherits the exact same pattern you drilled on day 10; treat it as the same chunk with a `k` added.
