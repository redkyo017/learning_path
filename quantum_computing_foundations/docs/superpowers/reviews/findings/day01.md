# Day 01 findings
## Correctness
- [01-C1] CRITICAL — content/day01.md:229 — Solution 4 says computing $\neg a,\neg b$ in place needs "no new ancilla ... if you're willing to overwrite — though to stay strictly reversible you'd route these onto fresh ancilla lines" — this is false: an in-place NOT (Toffoli$(1,1,\cdot)$) is a bijection, so overwriting $a$ with $\neg a$ is fully, strictly reversible; the day's own definition (line 32–35) makes reversibility = bijectivity, and Solution 9 correctly ties Landauer's bound to *many-to-one* overwrites only. A learner absorbs "overwrite ⇒ irreversible," which is exactly the misconception the day is built to dispel — fix: say the fresh lines are needed only to *keep $a,b$ available unchanged* for later use, not for reversibility.
- [01-C2] MAJOR — content/day01.md:144 — Worked example counts "one 'real' answer plus 5 garbage bits (three AND-results, two intermediate ORs) for a total of 9 output lines from 3 input lines" — the displayed formula $\neg(\neg g_1 \wedge \neg(g_2\vee g_3))$ produces only ONE intermediate OR value ($g_2\vee g_3$); with in-place NOTs the construction yields 4 garbage bits on 8 lines (3 data + 5 ancilla), and no clean variant reproduces "three AND-results, two intermediate ORs"; the constant-1 lines feeding the NOT-Toffolis are also uncounted — fix: pick one explicit gate list and count its lines exactly (e.g. 3 inputs + $g_1,g_2,g_3$ + 2 OR-stage ancillas = 8 lines, 4 garbage + 1 answer), and note the reusable constant-1 pair separately.
- [01-C3] MAJOR — content/day01.md:259 — Solution 7 says sum is "exactly Exercise 5's 3-bit XOR: 2 CNOTs, 0 ancillas, writing the result onto a fresh line" — self-contradictory: writing onto a fresh line IS one ancilla, and putting $a\oplus b\oplus c_{in}$ on a fresh $0$-line while preserving $c_{in}$ takes 3 CNOTs (one per input), not 2; the 2-CNOT/0-ancilla version necessarily overwrites $c_{in}$'s line — fix: state the two options separately with their true costs (2 CNOTs in place on $c_{in}$, or 3 CNOTs + 1 ancilla preserving all inputs).
