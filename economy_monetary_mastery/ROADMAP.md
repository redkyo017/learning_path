# Roadmap

| Phase | Goal | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Foundations (this course) | Mechanistic understanding + honest research-to-paper pipeline | In progress |
| 2 — Systematic investor | Asset allocation, rebalancing, tax-aware investing for a VN resident holding US + VN assets; refine the IPS | Not started |
| 3 — Deep quant | Statistical rigor, factor models, execution, small-capital live rollout with risk limits | Not started |
| 4 — Deep monetary theory | Monetary history, MMT/Austrian/monetarist debates, CBDCs, international monetary system design | Not started |

## Phase 1 — Foundations

**Entry criteria:** None. This is the starting point.

**Candidate topics:**

- Money, banking and central-bank balance sheets (Block 1)
- Bonds, discounting, portfolio theory and regimes (Block 2)
- FX, the dollar system and the Vietnamese regime (Block 3)
- Bitcoin, Ethereum, stablecoins, DeFi and crypto market structure (Block 4)
- Backtesting, bias, costs, sizing and strategy families (Block 5)
- Paper trading with a broker abstraction and kill switch
- Prediction journal and calibration
- Personal investment policy statement (first draft)

**Deliverable:** A capstone idea taken through the kill checklist to paper deployment, plus a first IPS and a Phase 2 plan.

**How to start:** Follow `README.md` Quickstart and start Day 1.

## Phase 2 — Systematic investor

**Entry criteria:** Phase 1 Day 30 capstone passed the kill checklist or was honestly killed; PROGRESS.md ≥ 90% ticked.

**Candidate topics:**

- Strategic asset allocation across US equities, bonds, gold and VN equities
- Rebalancing rules: calendar vs threshold, and their costs
- Tax treatment for a VN resident: dividends, capital gains, foreign-asset reporting
- Practical routes to US assets from Vietnam: brokers, funds, FX conversion and capital-flow rules
- Currency hedging policy for a VND-based investor
- Cash-flow planning, emergency reserves, and liability matching
- Behavioral guardrails: pre-commitment, drawdown rules, review cadence
- Refining the IPS with real constraints and a written review process

**Deliverable:** A tested, written allocation and rebalancing policy for a VN resident, with a costed implementation plan.

**How to start:** Paste the "start Phase 2 (Systematic investor)" prompt from `basic_prompt.md` at the repo root; it runs the building-learning-path workflow with this section as the brief.

## Phase 3 — Deep quant

**Entry criteria:** Phase 1 Day 30 capstone passed the kill checklist or was honestly killed; PROGRESS.md ≥ 90% ticked.

**Candidate topics:**

- Statistical inference for returns: bootstrap, multiple-testing control, deflated Sharpe in depth
- Factor models: value, momentum, quality, low volatility; factor timing skepticism
- Time-series methods: stationarity, cointegration, regime models
- Transaction-cost modeling and execution: market impact, order slicing, TWAP/VWAP
- Portfolio construction: shrinkage covariance, risk budgeting, constraints
- Backtest infrastructure: point-in-time data, survivorship-free universes
- Live monitoring: slippage tracking, alpha-decay detection, kill rules
- Small-capital live rollout with hard risk limits and a written go/no-go

**Deliverable:** A researched strategy portfolio running live with small capital under pre-registered risk limits and a monitoring dashboard.

**How to start:** Run the building-learning-path workflow with this section as the brief.

## Phase 4 — Deep monetary theory

**Entry criteria:** Phase 1 Day 30 capstone passed the kill checklist or was honestly killed; PROGRESS.md ≥ 90% ticked.

**Candidate topics:**

- Monetary history: gold standard, Bretton Woods, the Great Inflation, 2008 and 2020 responses
- Monetarist, Keynesian, MMT and Austrian views compared on testable claims
- Fiscal-monetary interaction: debt sustainability, fiscal dominance, yield-curve control
- Central bank digital currencies: designs, privacy, bank disintermediation
- International monetary system: reserve-currency dynamics, de-dollarization claims, SDRs
- Emerging-market monetary frameworks, with Vietnam as the case study
- Banking regulation: Basel capital and liquidity rules, deposit insurance, resolution
- Cryptoassets in monetary theory: Bitcoin as money, stablecoins and monetary sovereignty

**Deliverable:** A written, source-cited position paper comparing monetary schools against historical episodes.

**How to start:** Run the building-learning-path workflow with this section as the brief.
